# VR polish — 2026-09-20

This continues the working sky/tool build. Work is local and uncommitted;
the older checkout remains untouched.

## Changes

- Sprint's skid decision now uses the same camera-relative world heading as
  locomotion in first person. Its old fixed-camera comparison repeatedly
  requested TURN_DASH at certain headset headings. Speed, collisions, easing,
  intentional reversals, button mapping, and title behavior are preserved.
- The controller-attached net gets a -3000 binary-angle-unit trim (about 16.5
  degrees), canceling its sideways grip. Mesh and catch endpoints inherit the
  same correction in every net state. Other tools and hand fallback are unchanged.
- All five rock shapes, in summer and winter, get actual missing rear faces
  instead of a rotated duplicate: two to five triangles along the original
  open edges, with rear normals and projected UVs. Original vertices and
  collision shapes remain. The new display lists are cached and use the
  existing solid-buildings setting.
- Building shells reflect depth about the measured center rather than rotating
  180 degrees, preserving left/right asymmetry. This includes skeleton buildings,
  museum, police station, and wishing well. The inset, original front pass,
  material resets, and translucent-part exclusions remain. **Rear building
  fill is still approximate artwork**, and some details resemble mirrored fronts.
- Town residency prepares Kapp'n's dock from distant acres and keeps him and
  the boat alive. Island loads do not seed a town boat; existing boats do not
  reset connection state. Kapp'n gets separate heap memory, preserving all nine
  villager slots. The controller waits for other demos, derives trip origin from
  the boat's acre, handles missing boats, and clears only its own demo clip.
  Cleanup uses a cached name after freeing the actor, fixing a use-after-free read.
